Privacy Policy & Cookie Disclosures

Last Updated: March 2026 • Effective for all visitors and job candidates worldwide.

Summary of Our Privacy Commitments

  • No Candidate Resumes Sold: FreshCommits never collects, stores, or sells candidate resumes or personal dossiers. All job cards link directly to employer ATS portals (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday).
  • Google AdSense Compliant: We strictly adhere to Google Publisher Program Policies and disclose our cookie and advertising practices transparently below.
  • CCPA & GDPR Compliant: California residents and European users enjoy full data subject rights, including the right to opt out of targeted ad tracking.

1. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookie Disclosure

Google, as a third-party advertising vendor, uses cookies to serve advertisements on FreshCommits.com. Google’s use of advertising cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and other sites across the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting:

  • Google Ad Settings: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI): https://optout.networkadvertising.org/
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA): https://www.aboutads.info/choices/

2. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA) – Do Not Sell My Info

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have specific statutory rights regarding their personal information:

  • Right to Know: You may request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected, if any.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request deletion of personal technical logs collected through site usage.
  • Right to Opt Out of Sale/Sharing: FreshCommits does NOT sell or rent personal information to data brokers. You may opt out of third-party advertising cookie sharing at any time by contacting our compliance desk at privacy@freshcommits.com.

3. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for EEA & UK Users

For visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, FreshCommits processes technical telemetry under the lawful bases of legitimate interest (maintaining system availability and cyber defense) and consent (for analytics and non-essential cookies). Users have the right to access, rectify, or request erasure of their information, or lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

4. Information We Collect Automatically

When you browse FreshCommits, our hosting servers automatically record standard web server log entries, including IP address (anonymized for analytics), browser type, referring/exit pages, operating system, timestamp, and clickstream metadata. These logs are used exclusively for security monitoring, preventing automated scraping abuse, and optimizing site speed.
