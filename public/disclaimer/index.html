Publisher Disclaimer & Verification Standards

Official disclosure regarding listings, third-party trademarks, compensation figures, and advertiser relationships.

Non-Affiliation Notice

FreshCommits is an independent editorial career publisher. Unless explicitly stated, we are not directly affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the companies, employers, or trademarks referenced on this platform (including Google, Cloudflare, Linear, Supabase, Stripe, Paramount, or Elevenlabs). All trademarks, logos, and company names remain the property of their respective owners.

1. Nature of the Directory Service

FreshCommits serves as an indexed directory linking candidates directly to employer-hosted career applications. We do not participate in candidate interviews, hiring decisions, or employment negotiations. Inclusion in our directory does not guarantee employment or an interview.

2. Compensation & Salary Data Standards

FreshCommits adheres strictly to truth-in-compensation standards. Salary figures labeled as "Verified" come directly from employer disclosures filed under US pay transparency laws. Figures labeled as "Estimated" represent regional algorithmic benchmarks. Employers reserve the right to alter compensation offerings during hiring cycles.

3. Advertising & Commercial Relationships

FreshCommits is supported by digital advertising, including Google AdSense. Advertisements are clearly designated with "Advertisement" labels and maintained with substantial spacing from clickable navigation and application links. Commercial relationships never influence our editorial job verification criteria or 0–2 YoE experience filters.

4. Verification & Expiration Notice

While we perform routine automated checks across employer ATS portals, job openings can close or change without advance warning. If you discover an expired or inaccurate requisition, please notify our team via our Contact Page for immediate resolution.
