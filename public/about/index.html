About FreshCommits

Founded in 2026 • Independent Early-Career Software Engineering Directory

Our Founding Thesis

The tech industry job market is plagued by broken aggregators, misleading keyword scrapers, and ghost listings demanding "3–5 years of commercial experience" for supposedly entry-level roles. FreshCommits was built to fix this. We curate, verify, and monitor software engineering opportunities strictly tailored for engineers with 0 to 2 years of experience—including new college graduates, bootcamp alumni, self-taught developers, and transitioning apprentices.

1. The FreshCommits Verification Standard

Every opportunity published on FreshCommits undergoes automated structural checks and human editorial auditing:

Strict 0–2 YoE Experience Cap

We programmatically parse job descriptions to disqualify requisitions requiring senior leadership, multi-year commercial track records, or senior titles.

Direct Employer Routing

We never gatekeep applications. Every job link connects directly to official employer Applicant Tracking Systems (Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever, Workday).

Truth-in-Compensation Auditing

We strictly differentiate between legally verified employer salary bounds and regional algorithmic estimates to protect candidate transparency.

2. Editorial Independence & Quality Guidelines

FreshCommits is an independent publisher. We do not accept payment to rank low-quality postings or promote questionable recruitment agencies. Our 27+ comprehensive career guides, technical interview roadmaps, and salary benchmark studies are written by senior engineers to deliver actionable, data-backed value to job seekers.

3. Active Requisition Health & Expiration Tracking

Unlike legacy boards that leave filled requisitions online indefinitely to inflate inventory, FreshCommits runs routine ATS health checks. Requisitions that close on employer portals are swiftly marked as "Position Filled" and paired with active early-career alternatives.

4. Organizational Details & Contact

FreshCommits is developed and maintained as an independent career resource. For media inquiries, partnership requests, or employer verifications, visit our Contact Page or reach our editorial team directly at contact@freshcommits.com.
