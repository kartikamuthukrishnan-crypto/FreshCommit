1. Report a Misleading or Stale Job Posting

If an employer has marked a position as closed, or if a listed opening unexpectedly requires 3+ years of experience, please email us with the listing URL and company name. Our team will verify the ATS status within 12 hours and flag the listing as "Position Filled" to protect other applicants.

2. Employer Direct Postings & Verification

Are you hiring entry-level software engineers, apprentices, or new graduates? FreshCommits welcomes direct postings from verified hiring teams. All listings must provide transparent compensation, direct application destinations, and authentic early-career qualification thresholds.

3. Intellectual Property & Legal Notices
